But here's the important part for **your** question: **I did not find a prominent mainstream Apple-platform app outside the fortune-telling/astrology/psychic category for which a crystal ball is a strongly recognizable brand symbol.**

That's a useful distinction.

A crystal ball is obviously an established visual metaphor, so NextSeason wouldn't be inventing the symbol. But it doesn't appear to be "owned" by a major app in the way that, say, a camera aperture immediately runs into Apple's old Aperture identity. And most existing crystal-ball icons lean *very* hard into:

**purple + stars + sparkles + mystical glow + tarot/psychic aesthetic.**

That's exactly the territory I'd avoid.

For NextSeason, I think the interesting opportunity is to treat the crystal ball as **"a quiet glimpse into what's coming" rather than "I will tell your fortune."** That fits the product remarkably well: you put your shows in, and NextSeason quietly knows what's coming in the future. It also connects naturally to the calm/tranquil direction you liked earlier.

So I wouldn't reject the crystal ball because other apps use one. I'd instead make the design constraint:

> **It must unmistakably be a crystal ball, but it must not look like an astrology, tarot, psychic, or Magic 8-Ball app.**

That actually gives us a much more focused brief than most of the icon concepts we've tried so far. And looking at the existing icons above, there is **a lot of room to make a crystal ball look substantially more sophisticated and Apple-native than these do.**
